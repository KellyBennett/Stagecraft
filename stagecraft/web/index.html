Human alignment

Drag the orange guides onto two edges that run parallel in the scene along one ground direction. Drag the cyan guides onto two edges along a perpendicular ground direction. Each pair must converge toward its own vanishing point. Use straight architectural edges, not shadows or curves.

Place the purple origin on the ground below the horizon. This becomes world (0, 0, 0). Camera height sets scale. The camera assumes an uncropped pinhole image with its optical center at the image center.

Tab to select a handle. Arrow keys move it one pixel; Shift + arrow moves ten. Guide extensions show convergence. Saving resumes the waiting CLI.
